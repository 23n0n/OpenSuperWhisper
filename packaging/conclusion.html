OpenSuperWhisper is installed

The app is in /Applications, and everything it needs to run is inside it: the whisper speech engine and the llama.cpp transform engine are linked into the same binary, and neither one needs Homebrew, a background server or a listening port.

The transform model downloads on first use

Translation and tone need a language model (about 1 GB). It is not in this installer — every update would then carry it again. Turn on Translate Polish to English or Apply tone in Settings → Transcription, press Download model next to it, and the app fetches the weights into its own folder, verifies the published checksum, and keeps them there. Until then, dictation is pasted unchanged.

Uninstalling

One operation removes everything: the app, your dictation history, the downloaded models and the installer receipt.

  • Settings → Advanced → Uninstall OpenSuperWhisper…, or the same item in the menu-bar menu.
  • /Applications/Uninstall OpenSuperWhisper.command, if the app is already gone.

It leaves ~/models, /opt/homebrew and every other application's data untouched.
